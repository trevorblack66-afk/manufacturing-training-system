About This Project

I built this project to explore how simple digital tools can improve training consistency, qualification visibility, workforce development, and continuous improvement in manufacturing environments.

The project combines my interests in manufacturing operations, employee development, process improvement, and software development.

Rather than focusing only on tracking completed training, the system is designed around a broader question:

How can we make it easier for employees, trainers, and frontline leaders to understand what people know, what they are learning, and where support is needed?

All information shown in this demonstration is fictional.
